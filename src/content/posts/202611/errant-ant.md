[Errant Ant](https://errantant.com) is a reader for language learners. It counts every word you meet while you read and keeps the sentences you met it in, so a word comes back to you with its context instead of as a bare flashcard.
<!-- more -->

### Reading

Open your own EPUB, PDF or TXT books, or pick one from the Project Gutenberg catalog without leaving the app. Books are read page by page, and clicking a word opens a panel with dictionary entries, your notes, and the sentences where you have seen it before.

### Words

The words you meet build up as you read. Errant Ant shows how much of a text you already know, connects related words on a word map, and keeps your vocabulary of words and phrases. Word lists work with plain TXT, CSV and TSV files.

### Private by design

Errant Ant supports 12 languages and is local-first: no account, no ads and no analytics. Your books, reading progress, vocabulary and notes stay on your device, and dictionary lookups and translations run there too. It only goes online when you browse or download from Project Gutenberg, or download a dictionary or translation pack.

### Get it

Errant Ant is available for Windows from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PD02WCJVDQC), and a Steam release is coming soon. Find out more at [errantant.com](https://errantant.com).
